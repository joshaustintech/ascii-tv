# Glossary

Words this project uses on purpose. Where the code or an older conversation used a different word for the same thing, it is listed under _Avoid_.

## Place

A spot on the globe that **you** chose, with a label. It is marked on the globe while it faces the viewer. You can create one by picking a city or by giving custom coordinates.

_Avoid_: point, pin, location.

## Label

The text shown on the globe for a **Place**. Readable from across the room, so it is drawn larger than the globe's characters.

## City light

A lit mark on the night side of the globe standing for a populated city. Brighter and bigger for bigger cities. It stays fixed to the ground as the globe turns. It is shown for real cities, not scattered at random.

_Avoid_: night light, light pollution, lights (on their own).

## City

A real populated place that ships with the app. Cities are what **City lights** are drawn from, and what you choose from when creating a **Place**. A City is not a Place until you choose it.
